Privacy Policy – Roshnayi

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Roshnayi. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Roshnayi mobile application handles information.

2. Information We Collect

Roshnayi does not require account registration.

We do not collect or store personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party advertising SDKs.

3. Location Information

Roshnayi may ask for permission to access your device's location while you are using the app. This is used only to show the Qibla direction, to suggest your nearest city for prayer times, and to find nearby mosques.

Your location is processed on your device. We do not store it on our servers and we do not receive it. To show nearby mosques, the app uses Apple Maps search (MapKit), so an approximate location may be sent to Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Location access is optional. If you decline, you can choose your city manually. You can change this permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Local Data Storage

Application preferences and settings may be stored locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults and local storage.

This information is not transmitted to our servers.

5. Internet Access and Downloads

Roshnayi may connect to external websites to download Quran text, audio recitations, PDF documents, and other educational content (for example from api.alquran.cloud and islamhouse.com).

External hosting providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your device accesses their services.

Downloaded files may be stored locally on your device for offline access.

6. Notifications

Roshnayi may use local notifications for reminders and other application features.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

7. Photos

Roshnayi may ask for permission to add images to your Photos library, only when you choose to save a verse or hadith image. The app does not read or access your existing photos.

8. Third-Party Services

Roshnayi does not integrate analytics or advertising services such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or AdMob.

External content providers are subject to their own privacy policies.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Roshnayi does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

We rely on Apple's built-in device security features to help protect locally stored application data.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Updates will be published on this page.
